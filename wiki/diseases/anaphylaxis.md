# Anaphylaxis

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Anaphylaxis is a severe allergic reaction that can affect the airway, breathing, skin, stomach, and circulation.

## Common Symptoms
- hives
- rash
- swelling of lips, tongue, throat, or face
- wheezing
- shortness of breath
- vomiting
- dizziness
- fainting

## Risk Factors
- known severe allergy
- food allergy
- medication allergy
- insect sting allergy
- previous anaphylaxis
- asthma

## Warning Signs
- airway swelling
- trouble breathing
- wheezing after allergen exposure
- low blood pressure
- fainting
- rapid progression after exposure

## Common Investigations
- vital signs
- oxygen saturation
- airway assessment
- trigger history
- observation after stabilization

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Allergy and Immunology
- Intensive Care Unit if severe

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- asthma attack
- panic attack
- sepsis
- food poisoning
- angioedema

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway recognizes allergen exposure plus breathing, swelling, skin, or circulation symptoms and treats it as an emergency pattern.
