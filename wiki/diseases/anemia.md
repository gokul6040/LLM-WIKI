# Anemia

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Anemia means the body does not have enough healthy red blood cells or hemoglobin to carry oxygen effectively.

## Common Symptoms
- fatigue
- weakness
- pale skin
- shortness of breath on exertion
- dizziness
- fast heartbeat
- chest pain in severe cases
- cold hands and feet

## Risk Factors
- iron deficiency
- heavy menstrual bleeding
- chronic kidney disease
- chronic inflammation
- vitamin B12 deficiency
- folate deficiency
- blood loss
- cancer
- pregnancy

## Warning Signs
- chest pain with anemia
- fainting
- severe shortness of breath
- black stools
- vomiting blood
- very low hemoglobin
- rapid blood loss

## Common Investigations
- complete blood count
- hemoglobin
- iron studies
- vitamin B12
- folate
- kidney function
- stool blood testing if bleeding is suspected

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- Internal Medicine
- [[Hematology]]
- Gastroenterology if bleeding is suspected
- Emergency Medicine if severe

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- kidney disease
- heart failure
- hypothyroidism
- cancer
- nutritional deficiency
- GI bleeding

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway starts with blood count patterns, then looks for bleeding, nutritional deficiency, kidney disease, chronic inflammation, or bone marrow causes.
