# Preeclampsia

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Preeclampsia is high blood pressure during pregnancy with signs of organ involvement. It can affect the brain, liver, kidneys, blood, and placenta.

## Common Symptoms
- high blood pressure
- headache
- vision changes
- upper abdominal pain
- swelling
- shortness of breath

## Risk Factors
- first pregnancy
- previous preeclampsia
- multiple pregnancy
- chronic hypertension
- kidney disease
- diabetes
- older maternal age

## Warning Signs
- severe headache
- vision changes
- seizure
- severe upper abdominal pain
- shortness of breath
- very high blood pressure

## Common Investigations
- blood pressure
- urine protein
- platelets
- liver tests
- kidney function
- fetal assessment

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Obstetrics and Gynecology]]
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Maternal-Fetal Medicine
- Intensive Care Unit if severe

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- migraine
- hypertension
- kidney disease
- liver disease
- seizure disorder
