# Asthma

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Asthma is a chronic airway condition where the airways become inflamed and narrowed. It can cause episodes of wheezing, cough, and breathlessness.

## Common Symptoms
- wheezing
- shortness of breath
- cough
- chest tightness
- night symptoms
- symptoms triggered by exercise or allergens

## Risk Factors
- family history
- allergies
- eczema
- smoke exposure
- air pollution
- occupational triggers
- respiratory infections

## Warning Signs
- unable to speak full sentences
- blue lips
- drowsiness
- confusion
- silent chest
- low oxygen
- no improvement with usual inhaler

## Common Investigations
- oxygen saturation
- peak flow
- spirometry when stable
- chest X-ray if unclear
- trigger assessment

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Pulmonology]]
- Allergy and Immunology
- Primary Care

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- [[COPD / Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease]]
- pneumonia
- heart failure
- panic attack
- pulmonary embolism
- vocal cord dysfunction

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway focuses on severity: breathing effort, oxygen level, ability to speak, trigger history, and response to initial care.
