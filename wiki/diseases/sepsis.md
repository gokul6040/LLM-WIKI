# Sepsis

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Sepsis is a dangerous body-wide response to infection that can lead to organ dysfunction, shock, and death. It is treated as a medical emergency.

## Common Symptoms
- fever or low temperature
- fast heart rate
- fast breathing
- low blood pressure
- confusion
- reduced urination
- weakness
- clammy skin

## Risk Factors
- older age
- very young age
- weakened immune system
- diabetes
- chronic kidney disease
- recent surgery
- urinary catheter
- recent hospitalization

## Warning Signs
- infection signs with confusion
- infection signs with low blood pressure
- fast breathing
- reduced urination
- cold or clammy skin
- severe weakness

## Common Investigations
- vital signs
- blood cultures
- complete blood count
- lactate
- kidney function
- liver function
- urine test
- source-specific imaging

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Internal Medicine
- [[Infectious Disease]]
- Intensive Care Unit
- Surgery if source control is needed

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- pneumonia
- urinary tract infection
- meningitis
- abdominal infection
- skin infection
- malaria
- dengue

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway recognizes infection plus danger signs, checks organ function and circulation, identifies the likely infection source, and escalates if shock or organ failure is suspected.
