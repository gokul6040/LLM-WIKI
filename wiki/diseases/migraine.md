# Migraine

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Migraine is a headache disorder that can cause severe throbbing headache, nausea, and sensitivity to light or sound. It can be recurrent and disabling.

## Common Symptoms
- moderate to severe headache
- one-sided headache
- nausea
- vomiting
- light sensitivity
- sound sensitivity
- visual aura
- worsening with activity

## Risk Factors
- family history
- female sex
- hormonal triggers
- stress
- sleep changes
- certain foods
- dehydration
- alcohol

## Warning Signs
- sudden worst headache of life
- new headache with neurological weakness
- headache with fever and stiff neck
- headache after head injury
- new headache in older adult
- headache with confusion

## Common Investigations
- clinical history
- neurological exam
- CT brain if red flags
- MRI brain if red flags
- blood tests in selected cases

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Neurology]]
- Emergency Medicine if red flags

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- stroke
- meningitis
- brain hemorrhage
- brain tumor
- sinus infection
- hypertension emergency
