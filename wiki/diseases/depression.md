# Depression

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Depression is a mood disorder with persistent low mood or loss of interest, often along with changes in sleep, energy, appetite, concentration, and self-worth.

## Common Symptoms
- low mood
- loss of interest
- sleep changes
- appetite changes
- fatigue
- poor concentration
- guilt
- hopelessness

## Risk Factors
- family history
- stress
- trauma
- chronic illness
- substance use
- postpartum period
- social isolation

## Warning Signs
- suicidal thoughts
- self-harm risk
- psychosis
- inability to care for self
- severe agitation

## Common Investigations
- clinical assessment
- screening questionnaire
- thyroid test if indicated
- complete blood count if fatigue is unclear

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Psychiatry]]
- Psychology
- Emergency Medicine if safety risk

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- hypothyroidism
- anemia
- bipolar disorder
- grief
- substance use disorder

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway screens safety first, then assesses duration, severity, functional impact, medical mimics, and mental health support needs.
