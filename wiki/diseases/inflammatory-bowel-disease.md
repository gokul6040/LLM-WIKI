# Inflammatory Bowel Disease

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Inflammatory bowel disease includes Crohn disease and ulcerative colitis. It causes chronic inflammation of the digestive tract.

## Common Symptoms
- chronic diarrhea
- abdominal pain
- blood in stool
- weight loss
- fatigue
- fever during flare

## Risk Factors
- family history
- immune dysregulation
- younger age
- smoking for Crohn disease
- previous episodes

## Warning Signs
- severe abdominal pain
- high fever
- significant bleeding
- dehydration
- toxic appearance

## Common Investigations
- complete blood count
- inflammatory markers
- stool tests
- colonoscopy
- imaging

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Gastroenterology]]
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Surgery if complications

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- IBS
- infection
- colon cancer
- celiac disease
- appendicitis

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway uses chronicity, blood in stool, inflammatory markers, stool testing, and colonoscopy to separate inflammatory disease from functional or infectious causes.
