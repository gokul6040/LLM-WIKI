# Gallstones / Cholecystitis

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Gallstones can block the gallbladder outlet and cause pain. Cholecystitis means inflammation of the gallbladder, often with fever and persistent right upper abdominal pain.

## Common Symptoms
- right upper abdominal pain
- pain after fatty meals
- nausea
- vomiting
- fever if inflamed
- pain radiating to shoulder

## Risk Factors
- older age
- female sex
- obesity
- pregnancy
- rapid weight loss
- family history

## Warning Signs
- fever with right upper abdominal pain
- jaundice
- confusion
- low blood pressure
- persistent vomiting

## Common Investigations
- liver tests
- white blood cell count
- bilirubin
- abdominal ultrasound

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[General Surgery]]
- [[Gastroenterology]]
- Radiology

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- pancreatitis
- hepatitis
- peptic ulcer disease
- heart attack

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway uses pain location, meal relation, fever, jaundice, liver tests, and ultrasound to route toward biliary or surgical evaluation.
