# Hypoglycemia

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Hypoglycemia means blood glucose is too low. It can affect the brain and may cause confusion, seizure, or unconsciousness if severe.

## Common Symptoms
- sweating
- shaking
- hunger
- palpitations
- confusion
- drowsiness
- seizure
- loss of consciousness

## Risk Factors
- diabetes medications
- missed meals
- excess alcohol
- kidney disease
- intense exercise
- critical illness

## Warning Signs
- confusion with low glucose
- seizure with low glucose
- unconsciousness
- recurrent episodes
- hypoglycemia in older or frail patient

## Common Investigations
- fingerstick blood glucose
- electrolytes
- kidney function
- medication review

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Endocrinology]]
- Internal Medicine
- Primary Care

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- stroke
- seizure disorder
- panic attack
- syncope
- intoxication

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway checks glucose early in confusion, seizure, collapse, or diabetic patients, then looks for medication, food intake, kidney function, and recurrence risk.
