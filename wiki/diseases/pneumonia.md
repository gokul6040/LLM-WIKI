# Pneumonia

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Pneumonia is an infection and inflammation of the lungs. It may affect oxygen exchange and can be mild, moderate, or severe depending on age, oxygen level, and other illnesses.

## Common Symptoms
- fever
- chills
- cough
- phlegm
- shortness of breath
- chest pain with breathing
- fatigue
- confusion in older adults

## Risk Factors
- older age
- young children
- smoking
- chronic lung disease
- weakened immune system
- recent viral illness
- aspiration risk

## Warning Signs
- low oxygen
- severe shortness of breath
- confusion
- low blood pressure
- blue lips
- persistent high fever

## Common Investigations
- oxygen saturation
- chest X-ray
- blood tests
- sputum test
- blood cultures if severe
- respiratory viral testing

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Pulmonology]]
- Internal Medicine
- [[Infectious Disease]]
- Intensive Care Unit

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- influenza
- COVID-like illness
- asthma exacerbation
- COPD exacerbation
- tuberculosis
- heart failure
- pulmonary embolism

## Typical Hospital Flow
The patient pathway usually checks breathing effort and oxygen level first, then uses chest examination, imaging, and severity assessment to decide outpatient care versus admission.
