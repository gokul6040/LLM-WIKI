# Angina

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Angina is chest discomfort caused by reduced blood flow to the heart muscle. It may occur during exertion or emotional stress and improve with rest, but new or worsening angina can represent an emergency pattern.

## Common Symptoms
- chest tightness
- chest pressure
- shortness of breath
- pain with exertion
- fatigue
- pain spreading to arm, jaw, or back

## Risk Factors
- coronary artery disease
- high blood pressure
- diabetes
- smoking
- high cholesterol
- older age

## Warning Signs
- chest pain at rest
- new chest pain
- worsening frequency
- chest pain with sweating
- chest pain with fainting

## Common Investigations
- [[ECG]]
- troponin if acute
- stress test when stable
- echocardiogram
- lipid profile

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Cardiology]]
- Primary Care

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- heart attack
- [[GERD / Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease]]
- panic attack
- aortic dissection
- pulmonary embolism

## Typical Hospital Flow
The key distinction is whether the pattern is stable or unstable. New, worsening, or rest pain is usually treated as a higher-risk chest pain pathway.
