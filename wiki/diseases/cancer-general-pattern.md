# Cancer / General Pattern

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Cancer is a group of diseases where abnormal cells grow uncontrollably and may spread to other parts of the body.

## Common Symptoms
- unexplained weight loss
- persistent fatigue
- unusual lump
- persistent cough
- blood in stool, urine, or sputum
- change in bowel habits
- unusual bleeding
- persistent pain

## Risk Factors
- age
- tobacco
- alcohol
- obesity
- certain infections
- radiation
- immunosuppression
- family history
- environmental exposures

## Warning Signs
- unexplained weight loss with persistent symptoms
- coughing blood
- blood in stool or urine
- persistent lump
- difficulty swallowing with weight loss
- non-healing wound

## Common Investigations
- physical examination
- blood tests
- imaging
- biopsy
- endoscopy depending on suspected site
- screening tests

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Oncology]]
- Surgery
- Radiology
- Pathology
- site-specific specialty

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- infection
- inflammatory disease
- benign tumors
- autoimmune disease
- nutritional deficiency

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway depends on the cancer site. Persistent unexplained symptoms usually lead to examination, targeted tests, imaging, biopsy, and staging if confirmed.
