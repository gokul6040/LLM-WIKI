# Influenza / Flu-like Illness

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Influenza is a respiratory viral infection that commonly causes fever, cough, sore throat, body aches, fatigue, and headache.

## Common Symptoms
- fever
- chills
- cough
- sore throat
- runny nose
- body aches
- headache
- fatigue

## Risk Factors
- older age
- very young age
- pregnancy
- chronic lung disease
- heart disease
- diabetes
- weakened immune system

## Warning Signs
- difficulty breathing
- chest pain
- confusion
- severe dehydration
- symptoms improving then worsening
- high-risk patient with worsening symptoms

## Common Investigations
- respiratory viral test
- oxygen saturation
- chest X-ray if pneumonia is suspected
- basic blood tests if severe

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Internal Medicine
- Pulmonology if severe

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- COVID-like illness
- pneumonia
- common cold
- dengue
- malaria
- sepsis
