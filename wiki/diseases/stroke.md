# Stroke

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
A stroke occurs when blood flow to part of the brain is blocked or when bleeding occurs in the brain. It is an emergency because brain function can be permanently affected.

## Common Symptoms
- sudden face drooping
- sudden arm or leg weakness
- sudden one-sided numbness
- speech difficulty
- confusion
- vision problems
- dizziness
- loss of balance
- sudden severe headache

## Risk Factors
- high blood pressure
- diabetes
- atrial fibrillation
- high cholesterol
- smoking
- previous stroke or TIA
- older age
- heart disease

## Warning Signs
- sudden one-sided weakness
- face droop
- speech difficulty
- sudden vision loss
- sudden severe headache
- loss of coordination

## Common Investigations
- neurological examination
- blood glucose
- blood pressure
- CT brain
- MRI brain
- [[ECG]]
- blood tests

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Neurology]]
- Stroke Team
- Radiology
- Rehabilitation Medicine

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- hypoglycemia
- seizure
- migraine
- brain tumor
- Bell's palsy
- vertigo

## Typical Hospital Flow
The early pathway focuses on recognizing sudden neurological symptoms, identifying the time symptoms started, brain imaging, and specialist stroke assessment.
