# Appendicitis

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Appendicitis is inflammation of the appendix and can require urgent surgical evaluation.

## Common Symptoms
- abdominal pain
- pain starting near belly button and moving to lower right abdomen
- loss of appetite
- nausea
- vomiting
- fever
- pain worse with movement

## Risk Factors
- younger age groups
- family history
- possible blockage of appendix

## Warning Signs
- severe abdominal pain
- rigid abdomen
- high fever with abdominal pain
- persistent vomiting
- fainting or low blood pressure

## Common Investigations
- physical exam
- blood tests
- urine test
- pregnancy test where relevant
- ultrasound
- CT scan

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[General Surgery]]
- Radiology
- Pediatrics

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- gastroenteritis
- kidney stone
- urinary tract infection
- ectopic pregnancy
- ovarian torsion
- inflammatory bowel disease

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway uses pain location, fever, vomiting, examination findings, urine tests, pregnancy context, imaging, and surgical consultation.
