# Malaria

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Malaria is a mosquito-borne parasitic infection that affects red blood cells and can cause fever cycles, chills, sweating, anemia, and severe illness.

## Common Symptoms
- fever
- chills
- sweating
- headache
- body aches
- fatigue
- nausea
- vomiting

## Risk Factors
- mosquito exposure
- travel or residence in malaria-endemic area
- lack of prevention where indicated
- pregnancy
- young children
- weakened immune system

## Warning Signs
- confusion
- seizure
- severe anemia
- difficulty breathing
- low blood pressure
- dark urine
- kidney injury
- persistent vomiting

## Common Investigations
- malaria blood smear
- rapid diagnostic test
- complete blood count
- liver function tests
- kidney function tests
- blood glucose

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Internal Medicine
- [[Infectious Disease]]
- Pediatrics
- Intensive Care Unit if severe

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- dengue
- typhoid
- influenza
- sepsis
- viral fever
- meningitis

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway combines fever with travel or endemic exposure, then uses malaria testing and severity checks.
