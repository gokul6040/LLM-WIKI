# Lung Cancer

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Lung cancer begins in lung tissue and may present with persistent cough, coughing blood, weight loss, chest pain, or shortness of breath.

## Common Symptoms
- persistent cough
- coughing blood
- weight loss
- chest pain
- shortness of breath
- hoarseness
- recurrent pneumonia

## Risk Factors
- smoking
- secondhand smoke
- radon exposure
- asbestos exposure
- air pollution
- family history
- older age

## Warning Signs
- coughing blood
- persistent cough with weight loss
- new hoarseness with cancer risk
- recurrent pneumonia in the same area

## Common Investigations
- chest X-ray
- CT chest
- bronchoscopy
- biopsy
- pathology

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Pulmonology]]
- [[Oncology]]
- Thoracic Surgery
- Radiology
- Pathology

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- tuberculosis
- pneumonia
- [[COPD / Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease]]
- heart failure
- lung infection
