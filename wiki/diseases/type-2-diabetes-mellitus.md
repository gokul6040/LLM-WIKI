# Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Type 2 diabetes is a long-term condition where blood glucose remains high because the body cannot use insulin effectively or cannot produce enough insulin.

## Common Symptoms
- frequent urination
- increased thirst
- increased hunger
- fatigue
- blurry vision
- slow wound healing
- frequent infections

## Risk Factors
- family history
- overweight or obesity
- physical inactivity
- older age
- history of gestational diabetes
- high blood pressure
- abnormal cholesterol

## Warning Signs
- confusion with high glucose
- vomiting with diabetes
- severe dehydration
- very low blood sugar symptoms
- deep or labored breathing

## Common Investigations
- random blood glucose
- fasting blood glucose
- HbA1c
- urine ketones if severe
- electrolytes
- kidney function tests

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Endocrinology]]
- Internal Medicine
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Nephrology]]
- Ophthalmology

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- diabetic ketoacidosis
- hypoglycemia
- kidney disease
- heart disease
- neuropathy
- retinopathy
