# Chronic Kidney Disease

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Chronic kidney disease means the kidneys are damaged over time and may not filter blood well. It often develops gradually and may have few symptoms early.

## Common Symptoms
- leg swelling
- fatigue
- nausea
- loss of appetite
- changes in urination
- shortness of breath from fluid overload
- itching
- confusion in advanced disease

## Risk Factors
- diabetes
- high blood pressure
- heart failure
- family history of kidney disease
- older age
- obesity
- previous acute kidney injury
- smoking

## Warning Signs
- very reduced urination
- confusion
- severe swelling
- shortness of breath from fluid overload
- very high blood pressure
- abnormal potassium

## Common Investigations
- serum creatinine
- estimated GFR
- urine albumin or protein
- urinalysis
- electrolytes
- blood pressure
- kidney ultrasound

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Nephrology]]
- Internal Medicine
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Dialysis Unit

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- diabetes
- hypertension
- heart failure
- acute kidney injury
- anemia
- electrolyte disorders

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway connects kidney function tests, urine protein, blood pressure, diabetes control, fluid status, and nephrology referral when advanced or worsening.
