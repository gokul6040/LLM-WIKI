# Hypertension / High Blood Pressure

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Hypertension means blood pressure is persistently higher than normal. It often has no symptoms but can damage blood vessels, heart, brain, kidneys, and eyes over time.

## Common Symptoms
- often no symptoms
- headache in severe cases
- dizziness
- chest pain
- shortness of breath
- vision changes

## Risk Factors
- older age
- family history
- high salt diet
- obesity
- physical inactivity
- alcohol use
- smoking
- diabetes
- kidney disease

## Warning Signs
- very high blood pressure with chest pain
- very high blood pressure with confusion
- vision changes
- stroke-like symptoms
- shortness of breath

## Common Investigations
- repeated blood pressure measurement
- kidney function tests
- urine test
- [[ECG]]
- cholesterol
- blood glucose

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Cardiology]]
- [[Nephrology]]
- [[Emergency Medicine]]

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- stroke
- heart failure
- kidney disease
- heart attack
- aortic dissection
