# Rheumatoid Arthritis

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Rheumatoid arthritis is an autoimmune disease that causes chronic joint inflammation and may affect other organs.

## Common Symptoms
- joint pain
- joint swelling
- morning stiffness
- fatigue
- symmetrical small joint symptoms

## Risk Factors
- family history
- female sex
- smoking
- autoimmune background

## Warning Signs
- hot swollen single joint with fever
- severe weakness
- chest symptoms
- eye pain or vision changes

## Common Investigations
- inflammatory markers
- rheumatoid factor
- anti-CCP
- X-rays or ultrasound
- complete blood count

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Rheumatology]]
- Primary Care
- Emergency Medicine if infection is suspected

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- osteoarthritis
- gout
- lupus
- septic arthritis

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway uses chronic inflammatory joint pattern, symmetry, stiffness duration, inflammatory markers, antibodies, and imaging.
