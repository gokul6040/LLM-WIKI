# Liver Cirrhosis

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Cirrhosis is advanced scarring of the liver. It can impair liver function and lead to fluid buildup, bleeding problems, confusion, and infection risk.

## Common Symptoms
- fatigue
- jaundice
- abdominal swelling
- leg swelling
- easy bruising
- confusion
- vomiting blood if varices bleed

## Risk Factors
- chronic alcohol use
- hepatitis B
- hepatitis C
- fatty liver disease
- autoimmune liver disease

## Warning Signs
- confusion
- vomiting blood
- black stools
- severe abdominal swelling with fever
- jaundice with severe illness

## Common Investigations
- liver function tests
- INR
- platelets
- ultrasound
- hepatitis tests
- endoscopy if varices are suspected

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Gastroenterology]]
- Hepatology
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Intensive Care Unit if bleeding

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- hepatitis
- liver cancer
- heart failure
- kidney disease
- alcohol-related liver disease

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway checks liver function, clotting, fluid buildup, confusion, bleeding, and cause of chronic liver injury.
