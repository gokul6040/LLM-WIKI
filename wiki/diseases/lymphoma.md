# Lymphoma

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Lymphoma is a cancer of lymphocytes. It may cause swollen lymph nodes and systemic symptoms.

## Common Symptoms
- swollen lymph nodes
- fever
- night sweats
- weight loss
- fatigue
- itching

## Risk Factors
- immune suppression
- certain infections
- family history
- older age depending on type

## Warning Signs
- rapidly enlarging nodes
- breathing difficulty from chest mass
- unexplained weight loss with fevers
- severe weakness

## Common Investigations
- physical exam
- complete blood count
- imaging
- lymph node biopsy
- pathology

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Hematology]]
- [[Oncology]]
- Primary Care
- Emergency Medicine if airway symptoms

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- infection
- leukemia
- tuberculosis
- autoimmune disease

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway focuses on persistent lymph node enlargement, systemic symptoms, imaging, and biopsy confirmation.
