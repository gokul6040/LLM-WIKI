# Pancreatitis

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Pancreatitis is inflammation of the pancreas. It commonly causes upper abdominal pain and can become severe.

## Common Symptoms
- upper abdominal pain
- pain radiating to the back
- nausea
- vomiting
- fever
- fast heart rate

## Risk Factors
- gallstones
- alcohol use
- high triglycerides
- certain medications
- previous pancreatitis

## Warning Signs
- severe persistent pain
- low blood pressure
- confusion
- persistent vomiting
- organ failure signs

## Common Investigations
- lipase
- liver tests
- electrolytes
- abdominal ultrasound
- CT scan if severe

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Gastroenterology]]
- [[General Surgery]]
- Intensive Care Unit if severe

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- gallbladder disease
- peptic ulcer disease
- heart attack
- appendicitis
- gastroenteritis

The pathway connects upper abdominal pain with enzyme testing, gallstone/alcohol context, hydration status, and severity assessment.
