# Heart Failure

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Heart failure means the heart cannot pump blood as effectively as the body needs. Fluid can build up in the lungs, legs, or abdomen.

## Common Symptoms
- shortness of breath
- shortness of breath when lying flat
- leg swelling
- fatigue
- rapid weight gain
- cough
- reduced exercise tolerance

## Risk Factors
- previous heart attack
- high blood pressure
- coronary artery disease
- diabetes
- kidney disease
- valve disease
- arrhythmias

## Warning Signs
- severe breathlessness at rest
- pink frothy sputum
- chest pain
- fainting
- confusion
- very low oxygen

## Common Investigations
- oxygen saturation
- chest X-ray
- [[ECG]]
- BNP or NT-proBNP
- kidney function tests
- electrolytes
- echocardiogram

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Cardiology]]
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Internal Medicine
- [[Nephrology]]

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- [[COPD / Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease]]
- pneumonia
- kidney disease
- pulmonary embolism
- anemia

## Typical Hospital Flow
The usual pathway connects breathlessness and swelling with oxygen assessment, chest imaging, ECG, blood tests, and evaluation of fluid overload.
