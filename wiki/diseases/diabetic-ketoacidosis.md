# Diabetic Ketoacidosis

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Diabetic ketoacidosis is a serious diabetic emergency where the body produces excess ketones and the blood becomes too acidic.

## Common Symptoms
- excessive thirst
- frequent urination
- vomiting
- abdominal pain
- deep breathing
- fruity breath
- confusion
- weakness

## Risk Factors
- type 1 diabetes
- missed insulin
- infection
- new diabetes diagnosis
- heart attack
- major illness

## Warning Signs
- diabetes with vomiting
- deep labored breathing
- confusion
- severe dehydration
- very high glucose with ketones

## Common Investigations
- blood glucose
- ketones
- electrolytes
- blood gas
- kidney function
- infection workup

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Endocrinology]]
- Internal Medicine
- Intensive Care Unit

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- sepsis
- gastroenteritis
- pancreatitis
- kidney injury
- hypoglycemia
