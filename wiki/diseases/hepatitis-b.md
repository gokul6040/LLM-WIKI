# Hepatitis B

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Hepatitis B is a viral infection of the liver. It can be acute or chronic and may lead to long-term liver damage.

## Common Symptoms
- fatigue
- jaundice
- dark urine
- abdominal pain
- nausea
- loss of appetite
- joint pain

## Risk Factors
- blood exposure
- sexual exposure
- birth exposure
- needle exposure
- unvaccinated status

## Warning Signs
- severe jaundice
- confusion
- bleeding tendency
- very abnormal liver tests

## Common Investigations
- hepatitis B surface antigen
- hepatitis B antibody tests
- liver function tests
- viral markers
- ultrasound if chronic

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Infectious Disease]]
- Hepatology
- [[Gastroenterology]]

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- hepatitis C
- cirrhosis
- liver cancer
- alcoholic hepatitis
- gallbladder disease
