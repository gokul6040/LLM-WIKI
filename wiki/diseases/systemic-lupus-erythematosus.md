# Systemic Lupus Erythematosus

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Lupus is an autoimmune disease that can affect many organs including skin, joints, kidneys, blood, lungs, heart, and nervous system.

## Common Symptoms
- fatigue
- joint pain
- rash
- photosensitivity
- fever
- mouth ulcers
- chest pain with breathing
- swelling

## Risk Factors
- female sex
- family history
- autoimmune background
- sun exposure triggers

## Warning Signs
- kidney symptoms
- chest pain
- neurological symptoms
- severe anemia
- high fever with immune suppression

## Common Investigations
- ANA
- anti-dsDNA
- complete blood count
- urinalysis
- kidney function
- complement levels

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Rheumatology]]
- [[Nephrology]]
- Dermatology
- Emergency Medicine if severe

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- rheumatoid arthritis
- viral illness
- kidney disease
- skin allergy
- anemia

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway looks for multi-system disease patterns, immune markers, blood abnormalities, kidney involvement, and skin or joint findings.
