# Acute Kidney Injury

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Acute kidney injury is a sudden decrease in kidney function. It can be caused by dehydration, infection, medications, obstruction, or poor circulation.

## Common Symptoms
- reduced urination
- swelling
- fatigue
- nausea
- confusion
- shortness of breath if fluid overloaded

## Risk Factors
- dehydration
- sepsis
- chronic kidney disease
- certain medications
- urinary obstruction
- heart failure
- recent contrast exposure

## Warning Signs
- very low urine output
- confusion
- severe electrolyte abnormality
- fluid overload
- sepsis signs

## Common Investigations
- creatinine
- electrolytes
- urinalysis
- urine output tracking
- kidney ultrasound
- medication review

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Nephrology]]
- Internal Medicine
- Intensive Care Unit if severe

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- chronic kidney disease
- dehydration
- sepsis
- heart failure
- urinary obstruction

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway identifies the pattern of kidney function decline, separates dehydration, obstruction, medication, and shock-related causes, and checks for dangerous electrolytes.
