# Ectopic Pregnancy

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Ectopic pregnancy occurs when a pregnancy implants outside the uterus, often in a fallopian tube. It can cause internal bleeding if it ruptures.

## Common Symptoms
- pelvic pain
- abdominal pain
- vaginal bleeding
- shoulder tip pain
- dizziness
- fainting

## Risk Factors
- previous ectopic pregnancy
- tubal surgery
- pelvic infection
- fertility treatment
- pregnancy

## Warning Signs
- positive pregnancy test with abdominal pain
- fainting
- shoulder pain
- low blood pressure
- heavy bleeding

## Common Investigations
- pregnancy test
- transvaginal ultrasound
- blood count
- serial hCG

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Obstetrics and Gynecology]]
- Surgery if unstable

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- miscarriage
- appendicitis
- ovarian torsion
- kidney stone
- pelvic infection

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway treats pregnancy status as a critical branch in abdominal or pelvic pain and uses ultrasound and hCG patterns.
