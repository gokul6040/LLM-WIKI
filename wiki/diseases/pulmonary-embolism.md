# Pulmonary Embolism

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Pulmonary embolism is a blockage in an artery of the lung, usually from a blood clot. It can cause sudden breathing symptoms and chest pain.

## Common Symptoms
- sudden shortness of breath
- chest pain worse with breathing
- fast heart rate
- coughing blood
- dizziness
- fainting
- leg swelling

## Risk Factors
- recent surgery
- immobility
- long travel
- pregnancy
- cancer
- previous clot
- clotting disorder
- hormonal therapy

## Warning Signs
- sudden severe breathlessness
- fainting
- low oxygen
- coughing blood
- chest pain with fast heart rate

## Common Investigations
- oxygen saturation
- [[ECG]]
- D-dimer in selected cases
- CT pulmonary angiography
- leg ultrasound

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Pulmonology]]
- [[Cardiology]]
- [[Hematology]]

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- heart attack
- pneumonia
- asthma
- panic attack
- pneumothorax

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway combines sudden symptoms with clot risk factors, oxygen level, ECG, and imaging decisions.
