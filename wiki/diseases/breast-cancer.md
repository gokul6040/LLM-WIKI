# Breast Cancer

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Breast cancer begins in breast tissue and may be found through screening or symptoms such as a lump or skin/nipple changes.

## Common Symptoms
- breast lump
- skin dimpling
- nipple discharge
- nipple inversion
- breast skin changes
- swollen lymph nodes

## Risk Factors
- older age
- family history
- genetic mutations
- hormonal factors
- radiation exposure
- obesity after menopause

## Warning Signs
- new hard lump
- bloody nipple discharge
- skin dimpling
- rapid breast change
- unexplained lymph node swelling

## Common Investigations
- clinical breast exam
- mammography
- ultrasound
- biopsy
- pathology

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- Breast Surgery
- [[Oncology]]
- Radiology
- Pathology

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- breast cyst
- fibroadenoma
- mastitis
- lymphoma

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway connects persistent breast changes with clinical exam, imaging, biopsy, pathology, and oncology or surgical planning if confirmed.
