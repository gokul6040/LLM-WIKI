# Leukemia

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Leukemia is a cancer of blood-forming tissues that can affect white blood cells and bone marrow function.

## Common Symptoms
- fatigue
- fever
- frequent infections
- easy bruising
- bleeding
- bone pain
- weight loss
- night sweats

## Risk Factors
- genetic factors
- radiation exposure
- certain chemicals
- previous chemotherapy
- some inherited syndromes

## Warning Signs
- fever with very low white cells
- severe bleeding
- very low hemoglobin symptoms
- confusion
- severe weakness

## Common Investigations
- complete blood count
- blood smear
- bone marrow biopsy
- flow cytometry
- chemistry tests

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Hematology]]
- [[Oncology]]
- Emergency Medicine if unstable
- Pathology

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- anemia
- infection
- lymphoma
- autoimmune disease
- bleeding disorder
