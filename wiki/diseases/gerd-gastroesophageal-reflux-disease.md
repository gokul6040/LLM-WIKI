# GERD / Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
GERD happens when stomach acid flows back into the esophagus, causing heartburn or regurgitation.

## Common Symptoms
- heartburn
- burning chest discomfort
- sour taste
- regurgitation
- chronic cough
- hoarseness
- symptoms worse after meals or lying down

## Risk Factors
- obesity
- large meals
- late meals
- smoking
- alcohol
- pregnancy
- hiatal hernia

## Warning Signs
- chest pain that could be cardiac
- difficulty swallowing
- unexplained weight loss
- vomiting blood
- black stools
- persistent vomiting
- anemia

## Common Investigations
- clinical history
- ECG if chest pain could be cardiac
- endoscopy if alarm symptoms
- pH testing in selected cases

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Gastroenterology]]
- Emergency Medicine if chest pain is concerning

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- heart attack
- angina
- peptic ulcer disease
- esophagitis
- panic attack
