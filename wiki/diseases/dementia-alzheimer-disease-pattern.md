# Dementia / Alzheimer Disease Pattern

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Dementia is a progressive decline in cognitive function affecting memory, reasoning, behavior, language, and daily activities. Alzheimer disease is one common cause.

## Common Symptoms
- memory loss
- confusion
- difficulty with familiar tasks
- language problems
- behavior changes
- getting lost
- poor judgment

## Risk Factors
- older age
- family history
- vascular risk factors
- head injury
- social isolation

## Warning Signs
- sudden confusion
- fever
- new weakness
- head injury
- unsafe behavior
- rapid decline

## Common Investigations
- cognitive assessment
- medication review
- thyroid test
- vitamin B12
- brain imaging in selected cases

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Neurology]]
- Geriatrics
- [[Psychiatry]]

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- delirium
- depression
- hypothyroidism
- vitamin B12 deficiency
- stroke

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway separates chronic progressive decline from acute confusion, then checks reversible contributors and safety needs.
