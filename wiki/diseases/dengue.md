# Dengue

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Dengue is a mosquito-borne viral illness. It can cause high fever, body pain, rash, and in severe cases bleeding or shock.

## Common Symptoms
- high fever
- severe headache
- pain behind the eyes
- muscle and joint pain
- rash
- nausea
- vomiting
- fatigue
- mild bleeding

## Risk Factors
- mosquito exposure
- travel or residence in dengue-endemic area
- previous dengue infection
- rainy season exposure

## Warning Signs
- severe abdominal pain
- persistent vomiting
- bleeding
- difficulty breathing
- extreme weakness
- restlessness
- cold or clammy skin
- low blood pressure

## Common Investigations
- complete blood count
- platelet count
- dengue antigen or antibody test depending on timing
- liver function tests
- hydration assessment

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Internal Medicine
- [[Infectious Disease]]
- Pediatrics
- Intensive Care Unit if severe

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- malaria
- influenza
- COVID-like illness
- typhoid
- sepsis
- viral fever
