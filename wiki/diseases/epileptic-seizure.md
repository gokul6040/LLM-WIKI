# Epileptic Seizure

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
A seizure happens when abnormal electrical activity in the brain causes changes in movement, awareness, sensation, or behavior.

## Common Symptoms
- jerking movements
- loss of consciousness
- staring spells
- confusion after episode
- tongue biting
- loss of bladder control
- sudden fall
- aura

## Risk Factors
- known epilepsy
- previous brain injury
- stroke
- brain infection
- brain tumor
- alcohol withdrawal
- low blood sugar
- electrolyte imbalance

## Warning Signs
- seizure lasting several minutes
- repeated seizures without recovery
- first-ever seizure
- seizure after head injury
- seizure with fever and stiff neck
- seizure during pregnancy

## Common Investigations
- blood glucose
- electrolytes
- toxicology if suspected
- CT brain if first seizure or trauma
- EEG in follow-up
- medication level if relevant

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- [[Neurology]]
- Pediatrics
- Intensive Care Unit

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- syncope
- stroke
- hypoglycemia
- panic attack
- heart rhythm problem
- meningitis

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway focuses on safety, glucose check, duration, recovery, possible triggers, and whether it is a first or repeated seizure.
