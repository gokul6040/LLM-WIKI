# Atrial Fibrillation

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Atrial fibrillation is an irregular heart rhythm. It can cause palpitations or breathlessness and is important because it can increase the risk of stroke.

## Common Symptoms
- palpitations
- irregular heartbeat
- shortness of breath
- fatigue
- dizziness
- chest discomfort

## Risk Factors
- older age
- high blood pressure
- heart disease
- thyroid disease
- sleep apnea
- alcohol use
- diabetes

## Warning Signs
- palpitations with fainting
- palpitations with chest pain
- stroke-like symptoms
- very fast heart rate
- severe shortness of breath

## Common Investigations
- [[ECG]]
- electrolytes
- thyroid tests
- echocardiogram
- Holter monitoring

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Cardiology]]
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Primary Care
- Internal Medicine

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- stroke
- heart failure
- hyperthyroidism
- panic attack
- pulmonary embolism

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway usually confirms rhythm on ECG, assesses rate and stability, checks for triggers, and considers stroke-risk context.
