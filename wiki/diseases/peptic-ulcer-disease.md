# Peptic Ulcer Disease

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Peptic ulcer disease involves sores in the lining of the stomach or upper small intestine.

## Common Symptoms
- burning upper abdominal pain
- nausea
- bloating
- early fullness
- black stools if bleeding
- vomiting blood if severe

## Risk Factors
- H. pylori infection
- NSAID use
- smoking
- alcohol
- serious illness

## Warning Signs
- vomiting blood
- black stools
- fainting
- severe sudden abdominal pain
- unexplained anemia

## Common Investigations
- complete blood count
- H. pylori testing
- stool blood test
- endoscopy if alarm features

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Gastroenterology]]
- Emergency Medicine if bleeding

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- [[GERD / Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease]]
- gastritis
- pancreatitis
- gallbladder disease
- heart attack
