# Colorectal Cancer

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Colorectal cancer starts in the colon or rectum. It may cause bleeding, bowel changes, anemia, weight loss, or abdominal symptoms.

## Common Symptoms
- blood in stool
- change in bowel habits
- abdominal pain
- unexplained anemia
- weight loss
- fatigue

## Risk Factors
- older age
- family history
- polyps
- inflammatory bowel disease
- obesity
- smoking
- alcohol

## Warning Signs
- blood in stool
- unexplained iron deficiency anemia
- unintentional weight loss
- persistent bowel change

## Common Investigations
- stool blood testing
- complete blood count
- colonoscopy
- biopsy
- imaging for staging

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Gastroenterology]]
- [[Oncology]]
- Colorectal Surgery
- Primary Care
- Pathology

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- hemorrhoids
- IBD
- IBS
- infection
- anemia

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway connects bleeding or bowel change with stool testing, colonoscopy, biopsy, and staging if cancer is confirmed.
