# Tuberculosis

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Tuberculosis is a bacterial infection that most often affects the lungs. It often has a longer symptom timeline than many common respiratory infections.

## Common Symptoms
- persistent cough
- cough lasting weeks
- coughing blood
- fever
- night sweats
- weight loss
- fatigue
- chest pain

## Risk Factors
- close contact with TB
- weakened immune system
- HIV infection
- crowded living conditions
- poor ventilation
- malnutrition
- travel or residence in high-burden areas

## Warning Signs
- coughing blood
- chronic cough with weight loss
- night sweats with fever
- severe breathlessness
- chest pain with systemic symptoms

## Common Investigations
- chest X-ray
- sputum test
- TB skin test or blood test
- microbiology testing
- HIV testing when appropriate

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Pulmonology]]

- Public Health

- Internal Medicine

## Related or Overlapping Conditions

- lung cancer

- fungal infection
- chronic bronchitis
