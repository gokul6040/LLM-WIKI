# COPD / Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
COPD is a long-term lung disease that causes airflow limitation, chronic cough, mucus production, and progressive breathlessness.

## Common Symptoms
- chronic cough
- phlegm
- wheezing
- shortness of breath
- fatigue
- recurrent chest infections

## Risk Factors
- smoking
- air pollution
- occupational dust exposure
- older age
- history of asthma
- genetic risk such as alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency

## Warning Signs
- severe worsening breathlessness
- low oxygen
- confusion
- blue lips
- chest pain
- fever with worsening cough

## Common Investigations
- oxygen saturation
- chest X-ray
- spirometry
- blood gases if severe
- [[ECG]]
- blood tests

## Commonly Involved Departments
- [[Pulmonology]]
- [[Emergency Medicine]]
- Internal Medicine
- Respiratory Therapy

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- asthma
- pneumonia
- heart failure
- pulmonary embolism
- lung cancer

## Typical Hospital Flow
The clinical pathway compares current symptoms with baseline breathing, checks oxygen, looks for infection or heart strain, and decides whether symptoms represent an exacerbation.
