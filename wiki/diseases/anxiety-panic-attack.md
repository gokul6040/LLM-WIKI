# Anxiety / Panic Attack

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Overview
Anxiety and panic attacks can cause sudden intense fear with physical symptoms such as palpitations, chest tightness, shortness of breath, sweating, and dizziness.

## Common Symptoms
- palpitations
- shortness of breath
- chest tightness
- sweating
- trembling
- fear of dying
- dizziness
- nausea

## Risk Factors
- stress
- trauma
- family history
- thyroid disease
- substance use
- sleep deprivation

## Warning Signs
- first episode with chest pain
- fainting
- low oxygen
- neurological symptoms
- suicidal thoughts
- substance intoxication

## Common Investigations
- clinical assessment
- ECG if chest symptoms
- thyroid tests if indicated
- substance screen in selected cases

## Commonly Involved Departments
- Primary Care
- [[Psychiatry]]
- Emergency Medicine if red flags

## Related or Overlapping Conditions
- heart attack
- asthma
- pulmonary embolism
- hyperthyroidism
- arrhythmia

## Typical Hospital Flow
The pathway avoids assuming panic until dangerous mimics such as heart, lung, rhythm, thyroid, or neurological causes are considered.
