# Fever Is Nonspecific

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Cross-Disease Reasoning

Fever can occur in many conditions, including pneumonia, influenza, dengue, malaria, sepsis, urinary tract infection, meningitis, appendicitis, cellulitis, and viral illnesses. Fever becomes more concerning when combined with confusion, stiff neck, low blood pressure, severe shortness of breath, rash with severe illness, severe abdominal pain, or persistent vomiting.
