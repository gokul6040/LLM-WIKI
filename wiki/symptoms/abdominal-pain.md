# Abdominal pain

> ⚠️ **Educational reference only.** This wiki does not diagnose conditions and does not prescribe treatment. If severe warning signs are present (chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness), seek urgent medical care.

## Symptom Notes

Commonly connected conditions:
- appendicitis
- gastroenteritis
- pancreatitis
- gallstones
- kidney stone
- ectopic pregnancy
- IBD
Interpretation:
Abdominal pain should be interpreted with duration, severity, age, vital signs, risk factors, exposure history, pregnancy status, medications, examination, and test results. The presence of one symptom alone is usually not enough to identify a disease.
