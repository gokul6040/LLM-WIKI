## Extract

**Main symptoms:** upper abdominal pain, pain radiating to the back, nausea, vomiting, fever, fast heart rate  
**Major warning signs:** severe persistent pain, low blood pressure, confusion, persistent vomiting  
**Key investigations:** lipase, liver tests, electrolytes, abdominal ultrasound, CT scan if severe  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, Gastroenterology, General Surgery, Intensive Care Unit if severe
