## Extract

**Main symptoms:** reduced urination, swelling, fatigue, nausea, confusion, shortness of breath if fluid overloaded  
**Major warning signs:** very low urine output, confusion, severe electrolyte abnormality, fluid overload  
**Key investigations:** creatinine, electrolytes, urinalysis, urine output tracking, kidney ultrasound  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, Nephrology, Internal Medicine, Intensive Care Unit if severe
