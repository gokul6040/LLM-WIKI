## Extract

Unexplained weight loss can connect to cancer, tuberculosis, HIV, diabetes, hyperthyroidism, inflammatory bowel disease, depression, chronic infection, and chronic organ disease. Persistent symptoms, bleeding, chronic cough, fever, night sweats, or lumps increase concern.
