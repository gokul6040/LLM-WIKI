**Main symptoms:** unexplained weight loss, persistent fatigue, unusual lump, persistent cough, blood in stool, urine, or sputum, change in bowel habits  
**Major warning signs:** unexplained weight loss with persistent symptoms, coughing blood, blood in stool or urine, persistent lump  
**Key investigations:** physical examination, blood tests, imaging, biopsy, endoscopy depending on suspected site  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Oncology, Surgery, Radiology
