**Main symptoms:** pelvic pain, abdominal pain, vaginal bleeding, shoulder tip pain, dizziness, fainting  
**Major warning signs:** positive pregnancy test with abdominal pain, fainting, shoulder pain, low blood pressure  
**Key investigations:** pregnancy test, transvaginal ultrasound, blood count, serial hCG  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, Obstetrics and Gynecology, Surgery if unstable
