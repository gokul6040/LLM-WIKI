## Extract

**Main symptoms:** fatigue, jaundice, dark urine, abdominal pain, nausea, loss of appetite  
**Major warning signs:** severe jaundice, confusion, bleeding tendency, very abnormal liver tests  
**Key investigations:** hepatitis B surface antigen, hepatitis B antibody tests, liver function tests, viral markers, ultrasound if chronic  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Infectious Disease, Hepatology, Gastroenterology
