**Major warning signs:** very high blood pressure with chest pain, very high blood pressure with confusion, vision changes, stroke-like symptoms  
**Key investigations:** repeated blood pressure measurement, kidney function tests, urine test, ECG, cholesterol  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Cardiology, Nephrology, Emergency Medicine
