## Extract

**Main symptoms:** sudden shortness of breath, chest pain worse with breathing, fast heart rate, coughing blood, dizziness, fainting  
**Major warning signs:** sudden severe breathlessness, fainting, low oxygen, coughing blood  
**Key investigations:** oxygen saturation, ECG, D-dimer in selected cases, CT pulmonary angiography, leg ultrasound  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, Pulmonology, Cardiology, Hematology
