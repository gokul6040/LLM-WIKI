## Extract

**Main symptoms:** heartburn, burning chest discomfort, sour taste, regurgitation, chronic cough, hoarseness  
**Major warning signs:** chest pain that could be cardiac, difficulty swallowing, unexplained weight loss, vomiting blood  
**Key investigations:** clinical history, ECG if chest pain could be cardiac, endoscopy if alarm symptoms, pH testing in selected cases  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Gastroenterology, Emergency Medicine if chest pain is concerning
