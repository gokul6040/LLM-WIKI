## Extract

**Main symptoms:** burning urination, frequent urination, urgent urination, lower abdominal pain, cloudy urine, fever if kidney involvement  
**Major warning signs:** fever with flank pain, confusion in older adult, low blood pressure, pregnancy with urinary symptoms  
**Key investigations:** urinalysis, urine culture, kidney function if severe, imaging if complicated  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Emergency Medicine, Urology, Internal Medicine
