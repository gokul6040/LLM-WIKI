## Extract

**Main symptoms:** fatigue, joint pain, rash, photosensitivity, fever, mouth ulcers  
**Major warning signs:** kidney symptoms, chest pain, neurological symptoms, severe anemia  
**Key investigations:** ANA, anti-dsDNA, complete blood count, urinalysis, kidney function  
**Departments:** Rheumatology, Nephrology, Dermatology, Emergency Medicine if severe
