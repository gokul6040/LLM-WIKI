**Main symptoms:** chronic cough, phlegm, wheezing, shortness of breath, fatigue, recurrent chest infections  
**Major warning signs:** severe worsening breathlessness, low oxygen, confusion, blue lips  
**Key investigations:** oxygen saturation, chest X-ray, spirometry, blood gases if severe, ECG  
**Departments:** Pulmonology, Emergency Medicine, Internal Medicine, Respiratory Therapy
