## Extract

This file is a general educational reference about common diseases, symptoms, warning signs, investigations, and hospital department routing. It is not a substitute for medical care, diagnosis, or treatment. Real symptoms should be discussed with a licensed healthcare professional. Severe symptoms such as chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, fainting, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, or confusion with illness require urgent medical attention.
