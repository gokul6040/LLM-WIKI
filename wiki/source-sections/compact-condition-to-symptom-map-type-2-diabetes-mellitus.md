## Extract

**Main symptoms:** frequent urination, increased thirst, increased hunger, fatigue, blurry vision, slow wound healing  
**Major warning signs:** confusion with high glucose, vomiting with diabetes, severe dehydration, very low blood sugar symptoms  
**Key investigations:** random blood glucose, fasting blood glucose, HbA1c, urine ketones if severe, electrolytes  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Endocrinology, Internal Medicine, Emergency Medicine
