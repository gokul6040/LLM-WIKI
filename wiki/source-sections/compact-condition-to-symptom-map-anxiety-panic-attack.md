## Extract

**Main symptoms:** palpitations, shortness of breath, chest tightness, sweating, trembling, fear of dying  
**Major warning signs:** first episode with chest pain, fainting, low oxygen, neurological symptoms  
**Key investigations:** clinical assessment, ECG if chest symptoms, thyroid tests if indicated, substance screen in selected cases  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Psychiatry, Emergency Medicine if red flags
