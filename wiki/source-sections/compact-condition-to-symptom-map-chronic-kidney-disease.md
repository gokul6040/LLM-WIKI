## Extract

**Main symptoms:** leg swelling, fatigue, nausea, loss of appetite, changes in urination, shortness of breath from fluid overload  
**Major warning signs:** very reduced urination, confusion, severe swelling, shortness of breath from fluid overload  
**Key investigations:** serum creatinine, estimated GFR, urine albumin or protein, urinalysis, electrolytes  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Nephrology, Internal Medicine, Emergency Medicine
