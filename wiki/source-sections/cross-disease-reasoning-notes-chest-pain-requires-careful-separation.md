## Extract

Chest pain can come from heart disease, lungs, stomach acid reflux, muscles, anxiety, blood clots, or aortic disease. Dangerous patterns include chest pain with sweating, breathlessness, fainting, radiation to the arm or jaw, sudden tearing quality, or major cardiovascular risk factors.
