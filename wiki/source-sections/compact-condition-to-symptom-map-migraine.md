## Extract

**Main symptoms:** moderate to severe headache, one-sided headache, nausea, vomiting, light sensitivity, sound sensitivity  
**Major warning signs:** sudden worst headache of life, new headache with neurological weakness, headache with fever and stiff neck, headache after head injury  
**Key investigations:** clinical history, neurological exam, CT brain if red flags, MRI brain if red flags, blood tests in selected cases  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Neurology, Emergency Medicine if red flags
