`Practical Example Questions > Which symptoms suggest stroke?`

## Extract

Stroke warning signs include sudden face drooping, sudden arm or leg weakness, sudden one-sided numbness, speech difficulty, confusion, trouble seeing, trouble walking, dizziness, loss of balance, or sudden severe headache.
