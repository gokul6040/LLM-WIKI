## Extract

Abdominal pain should be interpreted by location, duration, severity, vomiting, fever, bowel changes, urinary symptoms, pregnancy status, and whether the abdomen is rigid or the patient is fainting. Appendicitis, ectopic pregnancy, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, kidney stone, ulcers, and gastroenteritis may overlap.
