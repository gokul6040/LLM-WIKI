## Extract

**Main symptoms:** fatigue, fever, frequent infections, easy bruising, bleeding, bone pain  
**Major warning signs:** fever with very low white cells, severe bleeding, very low hemoglobin symptoms, confusion  
**Key investigations:** complete blood count, blood smear, bone marrow biopsy, flow cytometry, chemistry tests  
**Departments:** Hematology, Oncology, Emergency Medicine if unstable, Pathology
