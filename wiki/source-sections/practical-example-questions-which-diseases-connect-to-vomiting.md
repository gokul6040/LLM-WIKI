## Extract

Vomiting can connect to gastroenteritis, diabetic ketoacidosis, appendicitis, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, migraine, meningitis, pregnancy-related conditions, dengue warning signs, kidney disease, and medication or toxin exposure. Persistent vomiting, blood in vomit, dehydration, confusion, severe abdominal pain, or pregnancy makes it more concerning.
