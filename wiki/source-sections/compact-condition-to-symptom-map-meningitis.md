## Extract

**Main symptoms:** fever, severe headache, neck stiffness, confusion, vomiting, sensitivity to light  
**Major warning signs:** fever with stiff neck, fever with confusion, severe headache with rash, seizure with fever  
**Key investigations:** vital signs, neurological exam, blood cultures, lumbar puncture if safe and indicated, CT brain in selected cases  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, Neurology, Infectious Disease, Intensive Care Unit
