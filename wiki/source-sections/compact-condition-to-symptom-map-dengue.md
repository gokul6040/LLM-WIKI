## Extract

**Main symptoms:** high fever, severe headache, pain behind the eyes, muscle and joint pain, rash, nausea  
**Major warning signs:** severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, bleeding, difficulty breathing  
**Key investigations:** complete blood count, platelet count, dengue antigen or antibody test depending on timing, liver function tests, hydration assessment  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, Internal Medicine, Infectious Disease, Pediatrics
