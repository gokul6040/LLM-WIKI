## Extract

**Main symptoms:** fatigue, weakness, pale skin, shortness of breath on exertion, dizziness, fast heartbeat  
**Major warning signs:** chest pain with anemia, fainting, severe shortness of breath, black stools  
**Key investigations:** complete blood count, hemoglobin, iron studies, vitamin B12, folate  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Internal Medicine, Hematology, Gastroenterology if bleeding is suspected
