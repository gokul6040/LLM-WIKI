## Extract

**Main symptoms:** fever, chills, cough, sore throat, runny nose, body aches  
**Major warning signs:** difficulty breathing, chest pain, confusion, severe dehydration  
**Key investigations:** respiratory viral test, oxygen saturation, chest X-ray if pneumonia is suspected, basic blood tests if severe  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Emergency Medicine, Internal Medicine, Pulmonology if severe
