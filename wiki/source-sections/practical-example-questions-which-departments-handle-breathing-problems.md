`Practical Example Questions > Which departments handle breathing problems?`

## Extract

Severe breathing problems commonly start with Emergency Medicine. Pulmonology may be involved for asthma, COPD, pneumonia complications, tuberculosis, chronic cough, and lung cancer suspicion. Cardiology may be involved when heart failure, heart attack, or rhythm problems are possible. Allergy and Immunology may be involved for anaphylaxis follow-up.
