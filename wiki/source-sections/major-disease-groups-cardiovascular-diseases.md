## Extract

Cardiovascular diseases affect the heart and blood vessels. Important examples include heart attack, angina, stroke, atrial fibrillation, hypertension, and heart failure. Warning signs often include chest pain, breathlessness, fainting, sudden weakness, speech difficulty, or very high blood pressure with symptoms.
