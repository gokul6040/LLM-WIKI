## Extract

**Main symptoms:** persistent cough, cough lasting weeks, coughing blood, fever, night sweats, weight loss  
**Major warning signs:** coughing blood, chronic cough with weight loss, night sweats with fever, severe breathlessness  
**Key investigations:** chest X-ray, sputum test, TB skin test or blood test, microbiology testing, HIV testing when appropriate  
**Departments:** Pulmonology, Infectious Disease, Public Health, Emergency Medicine
