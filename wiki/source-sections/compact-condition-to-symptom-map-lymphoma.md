## Extract

**Main symptoms:** swollen lymph nodes, fever, night sweats, weight loss, fatigue, itching  
**Major warning signs:** rapidly enlarging nodes, breathing difficulty from chest mass, unexplained weight loss with fevers, severe weakness  
**Key investigations:** physical exam, complete blood count, imaging, lymph node biopsy, pathology  
**Departments:** Hematology, Oncology, Primary Care, Emergency Medicine if airway symptoms
