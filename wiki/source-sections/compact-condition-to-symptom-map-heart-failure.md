## Extract

**Main symptoms:** shortness of breath, shortness of breath when lying flat, leg swelling, fatigue, rapid weight gain, cough  
**Major warning signs:** severe breathlessness at rest, pink frothy sputum, chest pain, fainting  
**Key investigations:** oxygen saturation, chest X-ray, ECG, BNP or NT-proBNP, kidney function tests  
**Departments:** Cardiology, Emergency Medicine, Internal Medicine, Nephrology
