## Extract

**Main symptoms:** joint pain, joint swelling, morning stiffness, fatigue, symmetrical small joint symptoms  
**Major warning signs:** hot swollen single joint with fever, severe weakness, chest symptoms, eye pain or vision changes  
**Key investigations:** inflammatory markers, rheumatoid factor, anti-CCP, X-rays or ultrasound, complete blood count  
**Departments:** Rheumatology, Primary Care, Emergency Medicine if infection is suspected
