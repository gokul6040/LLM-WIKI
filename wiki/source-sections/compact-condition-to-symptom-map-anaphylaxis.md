## Extract

**Main symptoms:** hives, rash, swelling of lips, tongue, throat, or face, wheezing, shortness of breath, vomiting  
**Major warning signs:** airway swelling, trouble breathing, wheezing after allergen exposure, low blood pressure  
**Key investigations:** vital signs, oxygen saturation, airway assessment, trigger history, observation after stabilization  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, Allergy and Immunology, Intensive Care Unit if severe
