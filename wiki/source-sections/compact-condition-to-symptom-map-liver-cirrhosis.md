## Extract

**Main symptoms:** fatigue, jaundice, abdominal swelling, leg swelling, easy bruising, confusion  
**Major warning signs:** confusion, vomiting blood, black stools, severe abdominal swelling with fever  
**Key investigations:** liver function tests, INR, platelets, ultrasound, hepatitis tests  
**Departments:** Gastroenterology, Hepatology, Emergency Medicine, Intensive Care Unit if bleeding
