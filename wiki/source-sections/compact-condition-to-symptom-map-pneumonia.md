**Main symptoms:** fever, chills, cough, phlegm, shortness of breath, chest pain with breathing  
**Major warning signs:** low oxygen, severe shortness of breath, confusion, low blood pressure  
**Key investigations:** oxygen saturation, chest X-ray, blood tests, sputum test, blood cultures if severe  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, Pulmonology, Internal Medicine, Infectious Disease
