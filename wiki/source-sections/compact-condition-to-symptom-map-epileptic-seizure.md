## Extract

**Main symptoms:** jerking movements, loss of consciousness, staring spells, confusion after episode, tongue biting, loss of bladder control  
**Major warning signs:** seizure lasting several minutes, repeated seizures without recovery, first-ever seizure, seizure after head injury  
**Key investigations:** blood glucose, electrolytes, toxicology if suspected, CT brain if first seizure or trauma, EEG in follow-up  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, Neurology, Pediatrics, Intensive Care Unit
