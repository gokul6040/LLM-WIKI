## Extract

Gastrointestinal diseases include appendicitis, pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, ulcers, GERD, IBD, cirrhosis, and hepatitis. Red flags include severe abdominal pain, rigid abdomen, vomiting blood, black stools, jaundice with severe illness, persistent vomiting, or weight loss.
