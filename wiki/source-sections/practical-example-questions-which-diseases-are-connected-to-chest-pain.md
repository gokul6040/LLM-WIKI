`Practical Example Questions > Which diseases are connected to chest pain?`

## Extract

Chest pain is connected to heart attack, angina, aortic dissection, pulmonary embolism, pneumonia, GERD, panic attack, and muscle strain. The most urgent associated features are sweating, shortness of breath, fainting, pain spreading to arm or jaw, sudden tearing pain, or very high-risk cardiovascular history.
