## Extract

**Main symptoms:** high blood pressure, headache, vision changes, upper abdominal pain, swelling, shortness of breath  
**Major warning signs:** severe headache, vision changes, seizure, severe upper abdominal pain  
**Key investigations:** blood pressure, urine protein, platelets, liver tests, kidney function  
**Departments:** Obstetrics and Gynecology, Emergency Medicine, Maternal-Fetal Medicine, Intensive Care Unit if severe
