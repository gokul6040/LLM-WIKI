`Practical Example Questions > What diseases are connected to shortness of breath?`

## Extract

Shortness of breath can connect to asthma, COPD, pneumonia, heart failure, pulmonary embolism, heart attack, anaphylaxis, anemia, sepsis, and panic attack. Low oxygen, blue lips, chest pain, fainting, confusion, or airway swelling are high-priority warning signs.
