## Extract

Kidney and urinary conditions include chronic kidney disease, acute kidney injury, urinary tract infection, kidney stones, and complications from diabetes or hypertension. Warning signs include reduced urination, swelling, confusion, severe flank pain with fever, or abnormal electrolytes.
