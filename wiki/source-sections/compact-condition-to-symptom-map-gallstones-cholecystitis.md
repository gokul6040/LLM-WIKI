## Extract

**Main symptoms:** right upper abdominal pain, pain after fatty meals, nausea, vomiting, fever if inflamed, pain radiating to shoulder  
**Major warning signs:** fever with right upper abdominal pain, jaundice, confusion, low blood pressure  
**Key investigations:** liver tests, white blood cell count, bilirubin, abdominal ultrasound  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, General Surgery, Gastroenterology, Radiology
