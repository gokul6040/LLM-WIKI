## Extract

**Main symptoms:** abdominal pain, pain starting near belly button and moving to lower right abdomen, loss of appetite, nausea, vomiting, fever  
**Major warning signs:** severe abdominal pain, rigid abdomen, high fever with abdominal pain, persistent vomiting  
**Key investigations:** physical exam, blood tests, urine test, pregnancy test where relevant, ultrasound  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, General Surgery, Radiology, Pediatrics
