**Main symptoms:** burning upper abdominal pain, nausea, bloating, early fullness, black stools if bleeding, vomiting blood if severe  
**Major warning signs:** vomiting blood, black stools, fainting, severe sudden abdominal pain  
**Key investigations:** complete blood count, H. pylori testing, stool blood test, endoscopy if alarm features  
**Departments:** Primary Care, Gastroenterology, Emergency Medicine if bleeding
