**Main symptoms:** chronic diarrhea, abdominal pain, blood in stool, weight loss, fatigue, fever during flare  
**Major warning signs:** severe abdominal pain, high fever, significant bleeding, dehydration  
**Key investigations:** complete blood count, inflammatory markers, stool tests, colonoscopy, imaging  
**Departments:** Gastroenterology, Emergency Medicine, Surgery if complications
