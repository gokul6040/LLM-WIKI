## Extract

Infections can be local or systemic. Some infections remain mild, while others can lead to sepsis or organ dysfunction. Fever should be interpreted with other features such as cough, rash, travel, stiff neck, confusion, low blood pressure, abdominal pain, urinary symptoms, or breathing difficulty.
