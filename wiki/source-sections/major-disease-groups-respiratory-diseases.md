## Extract

Respiratory diseases affect the airways, lungs, and oxygen exchange. Important examples include pneumonia, asthma, COPD, tuberculosis, pulmonary embolism, flu-like illness, and lung cancer. Warning signs often include low oxygen, severe breathlessness, blue lips, chest pain with breathing, confusion, or coughing blood.
