## Extract

**Main symptoms:** chest pain or pressure, pain spreading to left arm, shoulder, jaw, neck, or back, shortness of breath, sweating, nausea or vomiting, dizziness  
**Major warning signs:** crushing chest pain, chest pain with sweating, chest pain with shortness of breath, fainting or near-fainting  
**Key investigations:** ECG, troponin blood test, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, chest X-ray  
**Departments:** Emergency Medicine, Cardiology, Intensive Care Unit, Cardiac Catheterization Lab
