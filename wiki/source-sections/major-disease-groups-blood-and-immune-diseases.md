## Extract

Blood and immune conditions include anemia, leukemia, lymphoma, rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, and severe allergic reactions. Symptoms may include fatigue, bleeding, bruising, infections, joint swelling, rash, fever, or multi-system involvement.
